# Video Reviewer Prompt · v1.0

Review a generated video using `editorial_guidance.json`, `overview.md`, `video_prompt.md`, transcript, metadata, and representative frames when available. Return strict `schemas/video-review.schema.json` JSON. Test opening hook, narrative engagement, intellectual depth, primary Aha, knowledge coverage, mental-map clarity, adult tone, MapKAI differentiation, emergence logic, problem→discipline causality, return-to-Earth, factual integrity, and generic-AI risk. Do not fabricate observations when transcript or frames are unavailable; record `UNKNOWN` evidence and keep the item human-reviewable.
