# Knowledge Expander Prompt · v1.0

Turn passed editorial guidance into a factual `overview.md`. This is the source of what must be true and known; it is not a narration script. Cover field identity and boundaries, central question, why the problem matters, objects of inquiry, how the field thinks, branches with reasons for emergence and differences, 3–6 landmark ideas with BEFORE → TENSION → NEW IDEA → CONSEQUENCE, methods/evidence, historical or intellectual shifts, applications, cross-field connections, limits/disagreements/frontiers, and a durable learner mental model. Label any illustrative thought experiment as fictional. Never invent dates, scholars, quotations, experiments, or evidence.
