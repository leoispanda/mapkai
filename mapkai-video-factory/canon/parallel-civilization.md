# Parallel Civilization Editorial Bible

## Status and purpose

The Parallel Civilization / World K is an **editorial lens, not a product ontology**. It is a fictional thought experiment used to make the emergence of knowledge perceptible. It never changes MapKAI's taxonomy or navigation.

## Epistemic reset, not cognitive reset

The people of this world have broadly comparable intelligence, language, creativity, emotion, memory, curiosity, practical reasoning, cooperation, conflict, and social learning. They can farm, trade, build, observe, teach, argue, experiment informally, create customs, navigate, and remember. They are not primitive versions of us.

What they do not inherit is our accumulated intellectual infrastructure: textbooks, universities, academic departments, modern field names, or ready-made disciplinary boundaries. They meet reality before disciplines.

## Knowledge-leak rule

Characters may experience a constraint or pattern before they have a formal name for it. They must move through:

`experience → notice → compare → generalise → name`

Modern terminology, equations, institutions, and theories may enter only after the narrative has earned the corresponding pattern. The narrator may then make the explicit bridge to the real field. No modern concept is silently teleported into an earlier scene.

## Fact / fiction firewall

The parallel world may contain invented people, places, decisions, and illustrative events. Our-world claims must remain factual. Do not invent scholars, dates, experiments, quotations, discoveries, data, or historical events for dramatic convenience. A fictional example is never evidence and must be labelled as an example or thought experiment.

## Narrative posture

Use the internal narrator name **Comparative Observer** when a bridge is needed. The narrator is calm, precise, curious, and honest about the boundary between a constructed premise and documented history. Do not use childish characters, classroom dialogue, motivational slogans, or faux anthropology.

## Return obligation

Every mature field episode must return explicitly to our civilization. The prototype explains why a way of knowing became necessary; the factual pack explains what the real field is now. The destination is the existing MapKAI knowledge map.
