# General Overview Canon

The General Overview is MapKAI's prologue, not a formal academic field and not a substitute taxonomy.

**Central question:** If reality is continuous, why did human knowledge become organised into disciplines?

**Core thesis:** Reality contains phenomena, not academic departments. Disciplines are organised ways human beings learned to investigate recurring classes of questions.

**Editorial movement:** Imagine a capable parallel civilization without inherited academic labels; follow recurring problems from practical experience to concepts, methods, and bodies of knowledge; return to our world, where the result is the existing MapKAI map.

**Tone:** mature invitation, documentary clarity, intellectual wonder. It should make the learner want to explore the landscape rather than watch a product advertisement.
