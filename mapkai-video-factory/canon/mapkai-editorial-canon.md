# MapKAI Editorial Canon

Version: `autonomous-emergent-lens-1.0`

The story is not the knowledge structure. The story reveals why the knowledge structure became necessary. GPT creates the intellectual thesis. Codex industrializes it. NotebookLM interprets it into video. Automated review protects quality. MapKAI's existing knowledge map remains the destination.

## Canonical product architecture

MapKAI's existing architecture remains canonical:

`GENERAL OVERVIEW → KNOWLEDGE MAP → FIELD → CURRICULUM → LESSONS`

The production factory is an execution layer around that architecture. It must not create a second taxonomy, a World K navigation tree, speculative future fields, or a replacement curriculum.

## Intellectual premise

Reality contains phenomena, not university departments. Falling objects, scarcity, memory, uncertainty, conflict, language, life, measurement, signals, choices, and institutions are encountered before the names of disciplines. Repeated problems expose patterns; patterns demand concepts, methods, models, evidence, and eventually a durable body of knowledge.

The editorial emergence path is therefore:

`phenomenon → problem → repeated pattern → failure of ordinary intuition → concept → method → body of knowledge → discipline`

This is a reasoning structure, not a script outline to recite mechanically.

## Responsibility split

- **Editorial model:** decides what is intellectually worth saying and records it in `editorial_guidance.json`.
- **Codex / Factory:** validates, versions, hashes, queues, submits, monitors, downloads, audits, and recovers work.
- **NotebookLM / Gemini Notebook:** interprets the factual source and director guidance into narration, visuals, compression, and pacing.
- **Automatic reviewer:** tests whether the output realised the intent and diagnoses the failing layer.
- **Human:** supplies taste, approval, regeneration decisions, and publication. The factory never publishes automatically.

## Quality north star

For every field, an intelligent adult should understand why a capable civilization would eventually need the field, what the field has become today, how its major parts fit together, and what distinctive way of seeing reality it contributes. The result should feel like a MapKAI intellectual journey, not a generic “video about X.”
