WHY ACCESS MATTERS

The value is in the exchange.

Trusted peers

A considered mix of senior decision-makers who bring relevant experience and make room for other perspectives.

Informed perspectives

Relevant journalism, public evidence and context can help members examine the question before the discussion.

Confidential dialogue

A setting designed for candid discussion, with clear expectations about confidentiality and attribution.

PROPOSED MEMBER EXPERIENCE

A useful conversation, prepared with care.

Three proposed services could help members discuss live strategic choices and continue exchanging experience over time.

01

Confidential roundtables

Small, facilitated discussions centred on strategic questions members are currently facing.

02

Journalism-informed briefings

Relevant reporting, public evidence and background context, with reported facts distinguished from analysis and member judgment.

03

Peer exchange and introductions

Ongoing exchange and introductions by mutual agreement, so members can find relevant experience and different points of view.

PROPOSED MEMBERSHIP AND SELECTION

A considered path into the circle.

This prototype describes a possible process. Submitting an expression of interest does not mean an invitation has been made.

  1. 01Expression of interest or personal referral
  2. 02Selection review
  3. 03Invitation
  4. 04Onboarding

What selection could consider

  • Senior decision-making responsibility.
  • Relevance of the challenges the applicant wants to discuss.
  • Willingness to contribute experience and listen to other perspectives.
  • Fit with the composition of the group.
  • Commitment to the proposed confidentiality expectations.

Proposed discussion expectations

Respect agreed confidentiality. Attribute remarks only with consent. Make introductions only when both people agree. This concept prototype does not establish formal legal confidentiality terms.
