Why has AI investment not yet delivered the expected productivity gains?

Companies have moved from experiments to deployment. The question is shifting from access to AI toward the harder work of redesigning processes, measuring returns, and managing uncertainty.

The answer cannot be found in one quarter or one company’s result. It requires context: what businesses expected, what they implemented, which constraints emerged, and how evidence changed over time.
