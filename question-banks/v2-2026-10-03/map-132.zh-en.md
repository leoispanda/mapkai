### 00-q1 · 单位价格

三包同品质麦片的规格与价格是：400克／3.20欧元，750克／5.25欧元，1千克／7.60欧元。只比较每公斤价格，哪包最便宜？

- A. 750克包装
- B. 1千克包装
- C. 400克包装

**答案：A。** 400克、750克和1千克包装的每公斤价格分别为8.00、7.00和7.60欧元。750克包装的标价不是最低，但换成相同重量后最便宜。

Three packs of the same-quality cereal cost: 400 g for €3.20, 750 g for €5.25 and 1 kg for €7.60. Which has the lowest price per kilogram?

- A. The 750 g pack
- B. The 1 kg pack
- C. The 400 g pack

**Answer: A.** The prices per kilogram of the 400 g, 750 g and 1 kg packs are €8.00, €7.00 and €7.60. The 750 g pack is not the cheapest at the checkout, but it is cheapest for an equal weight.

### 00-v2-q02 · 按比例调整数量

菜谱给两人用150克米。每人用量不变，改做五人份需要多少米？

- A. 225 克
- B. 300 克
- C. 375 克

**答案：C。** 人数从2人变成5人，是原来的2.5倍，所以米量也是150×2.5＝375克。增加人数要按比例缩放，不能只加一个固定数量。

A recipe uses 150 g of rice for two people. Keeping the amount per person the same, how much rice is needed for five people?

- A. 225 g
- B. 300 g
- C. 375 g

**Answer: C.** Five people need 2.5 times the amount for two people, so 150 × 2.5 = 375 g. Scale the quantity with the number of servings.

维修申请必须包含订单号、故障和希望对方采取的动作。订单号是A17，洗衣机漏水，你希望安排检修。哪封短邮件信息齐全？

### 00-q4 · 百分比基数

一件商品原价80元，降价25%，没有其他费用。现在应付多少钱？

- A. 20 元
- B. 60 元
- C. 64 元

**答案：B。** 折扣金额是80×25%＝20元，应付80－20＝60元。25%是减掉的部分，剩下的75%才是要支付的部分。

An item originally costs 80 units of money. Its price is reduced by 25%, with no other fees. What is the new price?

- A. 20 units
- B. 60 units
- C. 64 units

**Answer: B.** The reduction is 80 × 25% = 20 units, so the new price is 80 − 20 = 60. The 25% is the part removed, not the part paid.

### 01-q2 · 支架渐撤

学员已经能按步骤提示完成任务。老师想保持同一个任务，逐步减少提示，让学员独立完成。哪项安排符合这一做法？

- A. 保留原有提示，再增加相同任务的次数。
- B. 减少一步提示，让学员自己完成这步。
- C. 保留原有提示，再换成难度更高的任务。

**答案：B。** 逐步撤除支撑改变的是帮助量：让学员接过原由提示承担的一部分工作。增加重复或提高难度也可能用于教学，但不是这里要辨认的做法。

A learner can complete a task using step-by-step prompts. The teacher wants to keep the same task while gradually reducing help. Which arrangement follows that approach?

- A. Keep the prompts and increase the number of repetitions.
- B. Remove one prompt so the learner completes that step.
- C. Keep the prompts and move to a more difficult task.

**Answer: B.** Fading support reduces the amount of help so the learner takes over part of the work. Repetition or harder tasks can serve other teaching goals, but neither reduces the prompts.

事实核对：[IES WWC — Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1)。

### 01-q4 · 间隔练习

### 01-q6 · 范例与主动练习

### 01-q8 · 学习环境与激励

### 03-q10 · 发生率与报道量

- B. 100 ÷ 10000
- C. 200 ÷ 20

**答案：B。** 案件数100除以人口10000，对应一年每万人100起，或每100人1起。案件可能涉及同一人多次，不能把这个事件率直接说成1%居民遇案。200篇是报道量；200÷20是报道量倍数，都不是给定定义的案件率。

- B. 100 ÷ 10000
- C. 200 ÷ 20

**Answer: B.** The numerator is 100 cases and the population is 10,000: 100 cases per 10,000 residents that year, or one per 100. Repeated cases may involve the same person, so this is not the percentage of residents affected. The 200 reports and their tenfold increase are different quantities.

- C. 2.00 units cheaper per croissant

**答案：B。** 10:25加30分钟，最早10:55起飞，比10:40晚15分钟。原表有10分钟余量，吸收了25分钟到达延误的一部分。真实周转受多项安全与运营条件影响，这里的固定时长只用于教学。

In a teaching flight schedule, the same aircraft is due to arrive at 10:00 and depart again at 10:40. Required turnaround is fixed at 30 minutes. It actually arrives at 10:25; everything else is ready, and no replacement aircraft is available. What is the earliest departure delay relative to the plan?

- A. 10 minutes
- B. 15 minutes
- C. 25 minutes

**Answer: B.** Arrival at 10:25 plus 30 minutes gives 10:55, which is 15 minutes after the planned departure. The original ten-minute buffer absorbs part of the 25-minute arrival delay. Real turnarounds depend on safety and operational conditions; the fixed duration is only a teaching assumption.

### 10-q9 · 服务标准与需求适应

访客明确说无法听清口头说明，但能阅读文字。服务标准要求把领取地点、所需材料和截止时间全部说明。哪种调整既保留标准，又回应这位访客的需要？

- A. 用同一句口头说明反复朗读
- B. 把相同三项信息写在纸上提供
- C. 只写领取地点，省去另外两项

**答案：B。** 标准可以约束必须传递的信息，而不把口头渠道固定成唯一方法。改为完整书面信息符合既定标准，也回应沟通需要；只改变音量或删信息都没有同时满足条件。

A visitor says they cannot hear spoken instructions clearly but can read text. The service standard requires the collection location, required documents and deadline to be explained. Which adjustment preserves the standard and addresses this visitor’s need?

- A. Repeat the same spoken instructions several times
- B. Provide the same three pieces of information in writing
- C. Write only the collection location and omit the other details

**Answer: B.** A standard can specify required information without making speech the only channel. Complete written instructions preserve that information and meet the stated communication need. Repetition or omission does not satisfy both conditions.

### 10-v2-q10 · 道路行驶与空驶

一份教学出行记录中，网约车当天载客行驶800公里、空驶200公里；这些行程恰好替代原本600公里的私家车行驶，其余道路行驶不变。相比没有这些网约车行程，道路车辆行驶增加多少？

- A. 200公里
- B. 400公里
- C. 1000公里

**答案：B。** 新增800+200=1000公里，扣去被替代的600公里，净增400公里。空驶也占道路。这里仅计算车辆行驶距离；没有足够资料断定拥堵增加多少。

In a teaching travel record, ride-hailing vehicles drive 800 km with passengers and 200 km empty in one day. These trips replace exactly 600 km of private-car driving; all other road travel stays unchanged. How much additional vehicle distance is traveled compared with having none of these ride-hailing trips?

- A. 200 km
- B. 400 km
- C. 1,000 km

**Answer: B.** The service adds 800 + 200 = 1,000 vehicle-km and replaces 600, for a net increase of 400. Empty travel also uses road space. These figures describe distance; they do not establish the size of any congestion change.

### 10-q11 · 应急演练与响应证据

同一场馆进行了两次相同人数、相同情景的教学应急演练。改进交接流程后，模拟响应时间从8分钟变为5分钟，漏传信息从6项变为1项。哪项结论被这些记录直接支持？

- A. 该演练响应更快、漏传更少
- B. 该场馆真实灾害发生率下降
- C. 未测过的应急情景响应变快

**答案：A。** 记录直接显示该情景中响应更快、漏传更少，能用于继续改进准备。它没有记录真实灾害发生率，也没有检验所有情景；演练的价值不能用“灾害没发生”代替。

The same venue runs two teaching emergency exercises with the same number of participants and the same scenario. After revising its handoff process, simulated response time falls from 8 to 5 minutes and missed information items from 6 to 1. Which conclusion is directly supported by these records?

- A. Recorded response and handoffs improved in this drill
- B. Real disasters became less frequent at this venue
- C. Responses became faster in untested emergency scenarios

**Answer: A.** The records show faster response and fewer omissions in this scenario and can guide further preparedness work. They do not measure real disaster incidence or test every scenario. Exercise value cannot be inferred simply from a disaster not occurring.

### 10-q12 · 跨触点服务一致性

活动入口的书面路线把访客引向东侧大厅，广播却说活动在西侧大厅；两处各自清楚，但访客不断折返。为了消除这项矛盾，最应联合核对哪一组内容？

- A. 位置广播的播音音量和速度
- B. 书面路线与位置广播的内容
- C. 书面路线的文字字号和排版

**答案：B。** 访客跨触点使用信息，所以两条清楚但互相矛盾的消息仍会制造混乱。这里要核对同一到达任务的路线与广播，而不是单独提高某个触点的满意度。

An event’s written entrance directions send visitors to the east hall, while the announcement says the event is in the west hall. Each is individually clear, but visitors keep turning back. Which pair of aspects should be checked together to resolve this conflict?

- A. The volume and speaking pace of venue announcements
- B. The content of written directions and venue announcements
- C. The text size and layout of the written directions

**Answer: B.** Visitors use information across touchpoints, so two clear but contradictory messages still cause confusion. The relevant pair is the route and announcement for the same arrival task, rather than improving one touchpoint in isolation.
